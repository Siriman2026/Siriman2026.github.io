Dear Basketball

Publish:9/8/2026

By: Siriman Singare

Growing up in an African household wasn't really easy,

especially when thinking about playing sports.

I remember as a kid I alwayas dreamed of being in the NBA.

I would have a visions and see myself doing big things with

a basketball.

Basketball was more than just a game to me.

It was something that gave me HOPE, PEACE and MOTIVATION

A reason to keep working hard. every time I stepped onto the court.

I felt like i was one step closer to making my deam come true.
